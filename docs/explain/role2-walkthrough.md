# Role 2 walkthrough: Campus track + race rules (Noah Schlag)

This part decides where the player drives and when a lap counts. It holds the start grid, the checkpoints that must be crossed in order, the safe spots for recovery (key R) and the racing line that tells positions, the minimap and the bots how far along the lap every kart is. It also adds the white fence along the road, the Nova hall interior and a piano in the atrium that plays when a kart drives past.

## How it works
1. **Route data.** The race runs in Role 3's `Visuals/Scenes/Track_CampusWorld.unity`. Its route is `Visuals/CampusWorld/Data/track.json`: 697 centre points 2 m apart, 1,394 m, spawn at point 693 (`docs/route.md`). The layout comes from OpenStreetMap and the campus walk, generated by `tools/world_gen`; the first plan and the real distances are kept in `docs/route.md`.
2. **Race parts.** Role 3's `CampusWorldBuilder.AddRaceParts` puts a `TrackInfo` and a `TrackProgress` on the "Race" object, calls `SetRoadPoints` with the route, and makes one `Checkpoint` per entry in `track.json` with a box trigger (width from the file, 6 m high, 1 m deep) and a respawn pose 2 route points (about 4 m) further on.
3. **Set-up.** `RaceManager` checks the indices with `CheckpointValidation.FindProblem` (0..N-1, each once) and creates `new RaceRules(count)`. `CheckpointRelay` sits on every trigger and reports "the kart touched checkpoint N"; only the player's own kart counts (`RaceManager.IsKartCollider`).
4. **Lap rule.** `RaceRules` keeps one counter, `nextExpected`, starting at 1. `PassCheckpoint(i)` is true only when `i` is exactly that number; wrong order, backwards, a repeat, 0 or an unknown index return false and change nothing. Crossing checkpoint 0 calls `TryFinish()`, which is true only after N-1 checkpoints, and only once (`finished`). A missed checkpoint shows a hint: go back and drive through it.
5. **Recovery.** Key R calls `TrackInfo.GetRespawnPoint(rules.LastValidCheckpoint)`; the timer keeps running and no checkpoint is awarded. A missing respawn falls back to the spawn point.
6. **Racing line (T8).** On first use `TrackProgress.BuildLine` takes checkpoint 0's position and forward direction and calls `RacingLineBuilder.Build`: drop points closer than 1 cm, start at the point closest to the line, turn the list round if it runs against checkpoint 0's forward, keep one point about every 6 m on straights but every point where the road turns more than 4 degrees. The result goes into `Shared/TrackPolyline` (Role 4's maths); `TrackProgress` only forwards to it. RaceFlow picks it with `TrackProgressSource.For`, or a rougher line through the checkpoints if it is missing or unusable.
7. **Fence (T7).** `WhiteGuardrailBuilder.Build` reads `track.json`, lines the route up with the hall doors (`HallDoorApproach`, from `HallDoors.json`) and puts posts every 2.5 m, 0.3 m outside each road edge, 0.75 m high with two flat boards, plus an invisible 1.0 m collision wall. No fence in the tunnel; on the terrace deck only the invisible wall; in the upper plaza and the atrium the Nova Kart wall; inside the hall it follows `HallRailingCourse.json`. The prefab sits at 0/0/0, and Role 3's `NovaHallInsert` places it.
8. **Hall (T9).** `nova_hall.py` builds the Edificio Principal in its OpenStreetMap footprint and exports `NovaHallWorld.fbx` and `HallDoors.json`. `tools/world_gen/hall_route.py` writes its two indoor lanes into `track.json`, so checkpoints, progress line, bots and fence follow the lanes.
9. **Piano (T10).** The prefab sits at (39.72, 15.56, 83.74) with a 3D `AudioSource` (heard from 4 to 45 m). `AtriumPiano.OnTriggerStay` passes the flat distance, speed and time of any kart (`IKartState`, bots too) to `PianoReaction.Decide`: within 2.2 m at 1 m/s or more = crash (1 s pause), within 8 m at 3 m/s or more = chord (3 s pause), cycling C, F and G major. `PianoTone` makes the notes: four fading harmonics at 44,100 Hz, 4 ms attack, peak 0.8.

## Why it is built this way
- **Rules as plain C#, separate from the scene.** `RaceRules` has no MonoBehaviour, so Edit Mode tests check it in milliseconds. RaceFlow first ran against look-alike placeholders (decision 2026-11-08) and swapped to the real classes on 2026-11-10 (request Q5) without rebuilding scenes.
- **Checkpoint 0 = start/finish, indices exactly 0..N-1** (clarified in `docs/interfaces.md`, decision 2026-11-08). One counter is enough, and a missed checkpoint can still be fixed by driving back.
- **Track knows nothing about RaceFlow.** The trigger relay lives in RaceFlow; references point one way (`docs/architecture.md`).
- **`TrackProgress` wraps `TrackPolyline`** (round 2 contracts, decision 2026-11-11). Start and direction come from checkpoint 0, so the builder's point order cannot make the line start wrong or run backwards. Thinning keeps per-frame `DistanceAlongLap` cheap without cutting corners. Built on first use, so it does not matter whose `Awake` runs first.
- **Wide checkpoints at forks, none on a shortcut** (`docs/route.md`): both shortcuts are legal, and the racing line follows the main line.
- **Fence built from data by code.** Rerun after a new `track.json`; the prefab keeps its GUID. The terrace keeps only the invisible wall because its metal railings have no collider.
- **Piano sound made in code** (`PianoTone`): no sound file, so no licence question. The pauses stop a parked kart from playing it every frame.
- **Piano prefab is only a trigger and a sound.** The piano you see is part of the hall model, so the hall can be rebuilt without touching the sound code; the solid collider comes from the hall.
- **Markers instead of logic in `SpawnPoint` and `RespawnPoint`.** They only draw in the Scene view; RaceFlow reads plain transforms from `TrackInfo`, so a missing marker breaks nothing.
- **Grey box first, looks later** (decision 2026-11-07), to save time and AI tokens.

## Limits
- One lap only: `RaceManager.LapCount` returns 1, and `RaceRules` refuses everything after `TryFinish` until `StartLap` or `Reset`.
- Bots do not go through `RaceRules`; their laps are counted from jumps in `TrackProgress` distance (decision 2026-11-11).
- Distances are flat (x/z): a road over another road would confuse the closest-point search (the route has none). Fewer than 3 road points = `LapLength` 0 and the checkpoint fallback.
- Lap time not measured: 1,394 m at the 18 m/s top speed takes at least about 77 s, above the first target of 35-60 s (`docs/route.md`, task T6 still todo). T2 and T3 (grey box in `Track_Campus`) and T11 (volleyball) are todo.
- `hall_route.py` spreads 12 checkpoints; `pin_checkpoints.py` must run after `hall_join.py` (`docs/route.md`).
- The piano position is a constant in `AtriumPianoBuilder`; it moved once already when the hall changed.
- `WhiteGuardrailBuilder.cs` is 675 lines with later additions from other roles, the hardest file in the folder to read.

## How it could change
- More laps: `RaceManager.LapCount` and a lap counter in `RaceRules` (or `StartLap` after each finish).
- Highlight the next gate: expose `nextExpected` from `RaceRules` through a small `Shared` contract (task V13 in `TASKS.md` names this as the missing piece).
- Bots under the same rules: one `RaceRules` per `IRacer` and a relay that accepts every kart (`RaceManager`, `CheckpointRelay`).
- Height-aware progress for crossing roads: 3D distance in `RacingLineBuilder` and `Shared/TrackPolyline`.
- Read the piano position from the hall export (like `HallDoors.json`) instead of the constant in `AtriumPianoBuilder.cs`.

## Tests
- `Tests/EditMode/RaceRulesTests.cs` (13): in order then finish accepted; finish right after start, skipped, backwards, repeated, 0, -1 and out-of-range rejected; a missed checkpoint fixed by driving back; second finish ignored; `LastValidCheckpoint`; `Reset` after a finish; a track with only the line.
- `Tests/EditMode/RacingLineBuilderTests.cs` (9): start point first, reversed line turned round, no direction keeps order, duplicates removed, corners kept while thinning, no gap above spacing plus one point, null or too few points = empty, the component measures from the start, no points = no lap.
- `Tests/EditMode/AtriumPianoTests.cs` (12): fast pass plays, slow or far stays quiet, slow crash and reversing count, pauses, NaN input, volume 0.5 to 1.0, 440 Hz and octaves, notes never clip, fade out, broken input = silence.
- `Tests/EditMode/CheckpointValidationTests.cs` (Role 4, runs at set-up): indices 0..N-1 in any list order accepted; empty, numbered from 1, duplicate and negative refused.
- `Tests/EditMode/TrackPolylineTests.cs` (Role 4): the maths `TrackProgress` forwards to (lap length, projection, height ignored, wrap-around, duplicates, too few points).
- `Tests/EditMode/TrackProgressSourceTests.cs` and `TrackLayoutTests.cs` (Role 4): a track without `TrackProgress` uses the checkpoint line; empty or tiny lines are refused.
- `Tests/EditMode/NovaRouteMathTests.cs` (7): loop length, turn radius, slope, smoothing and closest point for the help scene.
- Play Mode `Tests/PlayMode/RaceSmokeTests.cs`: the race uses a campus `TrackInfo`, a clean lap finishes once, a skipped checkpoint does not finish, recover keeps time and progress, Retry resets.
- Not tested in code: fence look and collision, the hall, the piano by ear, how the route feels. Checked instead with Scene view gizmos (yellow racing line, green start ball, checkpoint boxes, piano spheres) and Role 3's playtests (V20, V21, V33).
